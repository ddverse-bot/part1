# OCT-MRI Registration 9.18 Yuntian

## Summary

I have implemented the registration method end to end. It takes an OCT block embedded in agarose and an MRI cropped around the block, and on the I58 brainstem pair it runs fully automatically. Checked by eye on the overlays, the brainstem body lines up within a few tenths of a millimetre. What remains is local tissue deformation that an affine transform cannot absorb.

## Method

The method has five steps.

1. Specimen mask. The agarose shows section stripes and tile seams, and each of them varies along only one array axis. Tissue texture varies along all three, so the mask keeps voxels with texture in every direction.
2. Score. Both volumes become soft bright/dark class maps, and the score correlates these maps plus the specimen outline. The only outline mismatch it counts is OCT tissue over MRI background, because the MRI crop can hold tissue that is not in the block. Swapping the two OCT classes flips the sign of the structure term exactly, so the sign gives the contrast polarity.
3. Search. The MRI is already cropped, so there is no whole-brain search, and the orientation headers of the two files fix left and right. For each of 8,000 rotations, all translations inside the crop are scored at once with FFT.
4. Affine refinement. The best 24 poses get a 12-parameter affine fit with a penalty on scale and shear, which assumes the voxel sizes in the files are right. Without the penalty the score shrinks the block to fit inside the MRI tissue.
5. Fine-structure refinement. The coarse score cannot settle the last few millimetres, so the best pose is refined on gradient directions inside both volumes (normalised gradient fields). These follow fibre bundles and vessels and need no intensity mapping. On I58 this step moves the block by 2.4 mm.

## Result on I58

The run takes about 11 minutes on one GPU with no manual input. The inverted contrast is detected automatically and the scales stay within 3% of 1. Plane by plane, vessels and fibre bundle edges in the brainstem body continue across the checkerboard with offsets of a few tenths of a millimetre. The parts of the method are all needed: with an intensity threshold instead of the texture mask, without the outline term or without the scale penalty, the block ends up 36 to 43 mm off. Two regions do not fit. The sections at one end of the block are 10 to 14% larger in plane than the MRI, off by up to 2 mm, and a few torn cerebellar pieces moved during sectioning. Neither is a pose error.
