# Talk notes — OCT to MRI registration (Aug 21)

*~3 minutes. Casual but precise. Screen: the Google Doc, scroll along.*

---

Quick update. The pipeline now runs end to end with no annotations and no position prior. It works on five subjects of the public Costantini data, and our own first pair arrived this week and is processing now.

**[Method, the flowchart]**

The whole thing is one idea. OCT and MRI look nothing alike, but both reduce to the same simple picture, white matter versus gray matter. Once each volume is a two class map they are directly comparable.

Step 1 gets us there. Each OCT z slice is normalized by its own tissue median, which takes out the depth sawtooth from the sectioning. The MRI is divided by its local mean, which takes out the coil bias field. That second one matters, after it a plain Otsu threshold agrees with the manual labels at Dice 0.9, up from 0.66. I trained a 3D U-Net as a check and it gives the same registration, so v1 ships with no training.

Step 2 is where the block actually gets found. We correlate the two class maps, scoring only inside the block mask so intensity scale does not matter. For a fixed orientation every translation at once is just an FFT, so we can afford to be exhaustive. Eight thousand orientations, each tested normal and mirrored, because some OCT stacks really are stored flipped. A whole hemisphere takes 150 seconds.

Step 3 refines the best candidates with an affine, coarse to fine, opening the degrees of freedom slowly. Rotation and translation, then one scale, then the full affine with the shears on a short leash.

Step 4 is vessels. MRI vessels are dark spots, OCT vessels we segment ourselves with Frangi, and correlating those as a third channel finishes the job. It is also the only thing that fixes the scale along the block's depth axis. On I46 that needed a 20 percent stretch, and the tissue geometry is completely blind to it.

**[Results, scroll through the figures]**

Every figure reads the same way. Where the block landed in the hemisphere, the raw OCT, the MRI resampled into that same plane by our transform, a checkerboard of the two, and the MRI edges drawn on the OCT. If the registration is right, anatomy continues across the checkerboard tiles and the orange edges sit on the OCT's own boundaries.

I46 and I55 have full annotations, Dice around 0.8 and 0.92. But the three big slabs are the ones I would look at. Fifty millimeter slabs dropped into a whole hemisphere with zero hints, and you can put B and C side by side and match them gyrus by gyrus.

**[What is new]**

Same data as Costantini, so worth saying what is different. Their registration needs manual gray matter, white matter and vessel segmentations on both sides, plus an MRI already cropped to roughly where the block came from. We removed both. Automatic structure maps replace the hand drawn labels, and exhaustive search replaces the crop. We also resolve which class is white matter and whether the stack is mirrored, and both of those flip from subject to subject.

One nice check. Their published affine has a 1.17 scale in depth, which they got from hand drawn vessels. We land on the same number automatically.

**[Our data, and close]**

Our first pair arrived, processing now, results next meeting. Worth noting it comes with no labels at all, which is exactly why the label free route was worth taking. Next two weeks, our own data down to sub millimeter, with the public subjects rerun as a regression check. After that the same machinery should carry over to OCT and LSFM.

---

*Did every public subject work?* Five verified on the overlays. The rest share one cause, on bath heavy MRIs the automatic foreground mask is not reliable yet, and a bad mask lets a flat region capture the match. First on the fix list, and nothing fails silently.

*How accurate really?* On I46 the manual vessel agreement reaches 47 percent within 150 microns, which is the ceiling the annotation itself allows. Random placement sits around 350 microns.

*Why not non rigid?* Tried it, worth about 0.015 Dice. Costantini also report that affine was enough for OCT to MRI.

*How will you score our own data with no labels?* Right now restart consistency and visual checks, 10 of 12 perturbed restarts return to the same pose. For a real number I would ask for about twenty matched anatomical points on both sides, roughly half an hour of annotation, and then we can report target registration error.
